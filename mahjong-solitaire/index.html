How to Play

Welcome to Mahjong Solitaire!

  • The Goal: Clear the entire board by matching pairs of identical tiles.
  • How to Match: Click on two tiles that have the exact same symbol and color.
  • The Catch: You can only select "Free" tiles. A tile is Free if it has no tiles sitting on top of it, and if it is not blocked on both the left and right sides.
  • Disabled tiles will look darker and cannot be clicked until you free them up.
